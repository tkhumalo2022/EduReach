Before you publish

Protect trust at every step.

Run through these checks for every new item. They keep EduReach accurate, accessible and respectful.

  • 01
    Facts verified

    Names, dates, qualifications, prices and claims are correct.

  • 02
    Consent confirmed

    Every learner or workshop image has the required permission.

  • 03
    Accessible content

    Images have useful alt text and headings are clear.

  • 04
    Draft reviewed

    Preview the item before selecting Publish in Wix.
